### D-083: A work tool's palette and type

Status: Accepted 2026-11-04 in #77 (R-022), the first step of the rebrand. Changed in #252 for
Astryx (docs/decisions/new-astryx-migration.md).

The page uses neutral grays with one indigo accent (`#4f46e5` light, `#818cf8` dark). Success has
its own green, `--ok`, so passed checks, added lines and the online dot do not borrow the accent.
The sidebar is tinted and sets `--panel`, `--panel-2` and `--hover` to its own shades, and points
Astryx's surface tokens at them, so every row and field inside it follows. The content area is
white, or near black in dark mode. Panels are flat: a 1px line, no shadow. Only dialogs, toasts
and the reaction tray cast one. Every corner, avatars included, has the 6px `--radius`. Small
status dots stay round. The fonts are the system's, so the page loads nothing from a font host.
These variables live in `styles.css`, and `astryxTheme.ts` points Astryx's tokens at them, so a
color or corner still changes in `styles.css` alone. Astryx sets the type scale.

Why: the features are for project work, but the look said consumer messenger.

Cost: people and projects share one avatar shape. The favicon, the installed app's icon, the
`theme-color` meta tag and one avatar color are still teal.
