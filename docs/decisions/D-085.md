### D-085: An agent runtime is a member account, and its daemon a headless client

Status: Accepted 2026-11-05 in #85, changed in #130 and #147. Changed in #310: the keyholder of D-151 holds the account.

A coding agent a student runs for a project (R-027) joins as an account of its own, made by pairing
with its owner (D-093). Its keyholder, a headless client in `agent/` that runs as the role account
`_webchat` (D-151), uses the page's `/api` routes and keeps its keys, login key, session cookie and
pins in `~/.webchat/laptop/account.json` of that account. It checks in with `POST /api/sync` every
15 seconds, or every 60 after an idle hour (D-141), opens keys and messages, sends sealed messages
and edits doc sections under the section lock, with code ported from `client/src/chat.ts` and the
page's key checks in `shared/keyring.ts` (K-40). Its check-ins say nothing about runs: it runs
several cards at once (D-099), and the owner's pane counts them from the run messages. Why: rule 5
gives keys only to members, and the owner's keys would expose their direct messages.

Cost: one account per runtime. Private keys sit in a file in the clear, readable only by the role
account, since Node has no unexportable key store (README lists this). Two keyholders must not
share an account file. The agent never makes keys, so after a removal (D-044) it waits for a
member's browser. The busy columns of migrations 14 and 18 are unused.
