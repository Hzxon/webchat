### D-117: The board is the source, and a linked repository keeps a copy

Status: Accepted 2026-11-07 in #167 (R-040), changed in #188.

The project's owner links its board to one of its repositories, sealed in the Roadmap's title. Their
page first imports the repository's entries from their agent, with their own numbers; a board card
whose number one takes moves to the next free one. From then on the agent keeps
`docs/roadmap.md` and `docs/decisions.md` as copies, rendered by `copyOf` in `shared/board.ts`, as
Copy as Markdown is: decisions in number order, the roadmap under Running, Ready, Ideas, Known
issues, Open questions, Dropped and Done, with Done and Fixed items as a table. After 10 quiet
minutes it commits them to `agent/board`, in one draft, never forced. A proposal file on main
reaches the owner as a draft, and `Claims K-12` in an open pull request sets K-12 `Running:
repo#N`. CI fails a hand edit of a copy. Why: the board and the repository drifted apart, and their
numbers clashed (#159).

Cost: card text is plain text in the repository; main lags the board until the owner merges; nothing
syncs while the laptop sleeps; a session that needs a decision number waits for the owner.
