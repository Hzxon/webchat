### D-021: The sync fingerprint is the change detector

Status: Accepted 2026-11-01 in 74d53ff. Changed in #310: the list follows `stamp` in `server/db/messages.ts`.

`db.stamp` builds one string per user from a single query. It covers the sum of the newest event
ids in their chats, the read and delivered pointers, how many chats they are in, the sum of the
times their members joined those chats, how many chats have keys, the sum of the times their keys
were replaced (D-044), a hash of who owns each of their projects and groups (D-045), how many people
there are and how many have keys, who is online, a hash of every agent with its owner and level
(K-41, D-089), a hash of the agents' repositories and which are online (D-094, D-103), a hash of
each doc's version, a hash of the doc locks held now (D-081), and which chats the user muted
(D-126). Owners go in as a hash, not a sum, so two handovers at once cannot cancel out. A check-in
whose stamp matches answers "unchanged" without loading anything else.

Cost: any new state that pages must see has to change this string, or pages miss it until
something else changes.
