### D-130: The sign-in screen tries Astryx; every other screen keeps styles.css

Status: Superseded by D-129 in #248 to #252, which moved every screen to Astryx with its reset. Proposed 2026-11-15 in #247 (docs/roadmap/new-astryx-pilot.md).

`AuthScreen` was built from Astryx components (`@astryxdesign/core` 0.7.16) under a runtime theme,
`client/src/astryxTheme.ts`, whose tokens point at D-083's variables. `layers.css` put `styles.css`
in a cascade layer under Astryx's, and Astryx's reset was left out. `App` loaded the screen lazily,
so only signed-out pages fetched Astryx. Why: to judge Astryx's look, its accessibility and its
cost on one real screen before moving any other.
