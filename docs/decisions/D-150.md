### D-150: The roadmap shows lanes, and Ready and Running are its words

Status: Proposed 2026-11-12 in #188 (docs/roadmap/new-board-lanes.md). Changed in #310: the four lanes of #299 (docs/decisions/board-redesign.md).

The Roadmap shows Idea, Ready, Running and In review as lanes, and Done only on request, newest
three first. Idea holds the open cards and starts folded into the header. Decisions are a tab
beside it. Pages write `Ready`, `Running: Ann's agent` and `Running: repo#N`, and still read `Next`
and `Planned` as Ready and `In progress` as Running. A card's owner is the author of its oldest
revision, when its sealed content says `opened`, which only `addCard` writes, and while they are a
member. `GET /api/docs/:id/words` returns each section's oldest revision and each author's newest,
so the page reads owners without the history. Why: nobody saw who opened a card or who works on it.

Cost: a server can withhold the marked first revision, so a card looks ownerless, and with a member
who signs a revision marked `opened`, present that member as owner. Cards from before, imported
ones and those whose opener left have none. Until it reloads (D-040), an older page sorts `Ready`
and `Running:` cards under Open, and its brief does not refuse a card that says `Running: Ann's
agent`.
